# Visual direction — Editorial Market Lab

## Decision

| Direction | Immediate understanding | Hierarchy | Pedagogical causality | Identity | Motion | Responsive | Weighted score |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **Editorial Market Lab** | 9.4 | 9.5 | 9.1 | 9.0 | 9.0 | 8.8 | **9.20** |
| Instrument Continuum | 8.9 | 8.8 | 9.7 | 9.4 | 9.5 | 8.2 | 9.11 |
| Financial Field | 8.1 | 8.3 | 8.7 | 9.3 | 9.2 | 8.0 | 8.54 |

Editorial Market Lab is the single V1 direction. Instrument Continuum has the
strongest transformation logic but becomes rigid in the YTM/CAGR comparison and
on mobile. Financial Field is powerful for the curve but too abstract for L1.

## Memorable device — Contract Spine

The same canonical bond keeps identity `B–04 / 5Y` throughout the block:

1. L1 — off-white contractual certificate.
2. L2 — its lower edge becomes the cash-flow timeline and PV contributions.
3. L3 — coupons travel to maturity and form terminal wealth.
4. L4 — certificates compress into observed `(residual maturity, YTM)` points.

The landing uses this same spine as its four-station map. Continuity carries
meaning; it is never ambient decoration.

One dominant object occupies at least 55% of the useful canvas.

## Composition

- Desktop canvas is 16:9 with a 12/76/12 vertical rhythm.
- The bond is material; observations and controls remain outside its contract.
- The timeline is full width and the final payment is 1.35× dominant.
- Formulas live in an editorial band, and the active term is spatially linked
  to the financial object it describes.
- The curve uses at least 70% of the useful width and separates 0–2Y exactly.
- At `≤900px`: claim → visual → control → result → takeaway; no horizontal
  scrolling and touch targets remain at least 44px.

## Anti-dashboard gate

- One dominant surface and at most one contextual band.
- Controls touch the variable they change.
- Maximum three focal groups.
- Annotations sit on the object or its margin.
- If hierarchy disappears when borders are removed, the composition fails.

## Source constraints

Five canonical WebPs are unusable at the verified baseline. Their composition is
reconstructed only from authoritative Markdown and repeated healthy patterns.
The runtime never embeds a reference WebP as a fullscreen background.
